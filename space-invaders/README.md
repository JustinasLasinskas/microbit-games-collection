# SPACE-INVADERS

## Description

A space invaders game where the player has to defend the bottom of the screen from waves of invaders that move across the screen and drop bombs.

## How to play the game

1. Press button A + button B to start the game.
2. Tilt the micro:bit left and right to move your ship.
3. Press button A or button B to shoot.
4. The objective is to shoot down every invader of the formation.
5. The invaders march across the screen and step down every third trip to the edge.
6. The invaders drop slow bombs, one invader at a time, destroy them before they reach your ship.
7. Your bullet is stopped by the top of the screen while any invader is still alive, so the top row is safe until the formation steps down.
8. Clearing a wave makes the invaders faster and their bombs more frequent.
9. The game ends if a bomb hits the ship or the invaders reach the bottom of the screen.
